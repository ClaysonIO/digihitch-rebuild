# Prince Edward Island (CA-PE)

**Status:** Restricted. "No person shall be on a roadway for the purpose of soliciting a ride" (s. 193(3), headed "Soliciting & hitch hiking prohibited"). PEI's "roadway" excludes the shoulder *unless the shoulder is paved*. An unpaved shoulder or the verge is the lawful place to stand. No provincial statute bans pedestrians from any class of highway.

## Summary

PEI's Highway Traffic Act has an explicit hitchhiking section, headed "Soliciting & hitch hiking prohibited". It only applies "on a roadway", and the Act defines roadway to exclude the shoulder "unless the shoulder is paved". On highways with paved shoulders, the paved shoulder counts as roadway.

What applies to hitchhikers:

- **Stand off the roadway.** Use a gravel shoulder, the grass verge, a sidewalk or a pull-off. Do not stand on a paved shoulder or in a lane.
- **Use the sidewalk** where one is reasonably passable (s. 193(1)).
- **No sidewalk:** walk on the left side of the roadway or shoulder, facing traffic, no more than two abreast (s. 193(2)).
- **No freeway ban.** The Act has a driver rule for entering and leaving controlled-access highways (s. 165), but no pedestrian prohibition. Traffic authorities may still make by-laws on "pedestrian traffic" (s. 310(1)(c)).

Penalty: a fine of $100 to $500 for soliciting rides on the roadway (Schedule, item 193(3)).

## Laws

### Highway Traffic Act s. 193: Pedestrians to use sidewalks; soliciting and hitch hiking

**Source:** [Government of Prince Edward Island, Highway Traffic Act, R.S.P.E.I. 1988, c. H-5 (office consolidation, PDF)](https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/sites/default/files/legislation/h-05-highway_traffic_act.pdf) (current to May 29, 2026)

> 193. Pedestrians to use sidewalks
> (1) Where there is a sidewalk that is reasonably passable on either or both sides of a highway, a pedestrian shall not walk on a roadway.
>
> Pedestrians on roadway, general rules
> (2) Where there is no sidewalk, a pedestrian walking along or upon a roadway or the shoulder thereof shall when practicable walk only on the left side of the roadway or the shoulder of the highway facing traffic approaching from the opposite direction and no more than two persons shall walk abreast on the roadway.
>
> Soliciting & hitch hiking prohibited
> (3) No person shall be on a roadway for the purpose of soliciting a ride, employment or business from the occupant of a vehicle. R.S.P.E.I. 1974, Cap. H-6, s.178.

**What it means:** Thumbing while standing on the roadway is an offence. The roadway definition below decides whether the shoulder counts. When walking, keep left and face traffic.

### Highway Traffic Act s. 1: Definitions

**Source:** [Government of Prince Edward Island, Highway Traffic Act, s. 1](https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/sites/default/files/legislation/h-05-highway_traffic_act.pdf)

> (c) "controlled access highway" means a highway
> (i) on to which persons have a right to enter from abutting land, and
> (ii) from which persons have a right to enter onto abutting land,
> only at fixed locations;
>
> (h.1) "highway" means the entire width between the boundary lines of every road, street, lane, alley, or right-of-way designed or intended for or used by the general public for the passage of vehicles, and includes any bridges over which every such road, street, lane, alley, or right-of-way is laid, and every private place or passage way to which the public, for the purpose of the parking or servicing of vehicles, has access or is invited;
>
> (q.3) "roadway" means that portion of a highway improved, designed or ordinarily used for vehicular travel, exclusive of the shoulder unless the shoulder is paved, and where a highway includes two or more separate roadways the term "roadway" refers to any one roadway separately and not to all of the roadways collectively;

**What it means:** On PEI a paved shoulder is part of the roadway, so s. 193(3) applies there. A gravel shoulder is not. Gas-station forecourts and parking areas open to the public count as "highway", but not as "roadway".

### Highway Traffic Act s. 300 and Schedule: Penalties

**Source:** [Government of Prince Edward Island, Highway Traffic Act, s. 300 and Schedule](https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/sites/default/files/legislation/h-05-highway_traffic_act.pdf)

> 300. Application of penalties in Schedule
> (1) Unless otherwise provided by this Act, any person convicted of an offence against this Act shall be adjudged to pay a fine of an amount not more than the maximum nor less than the minimum amount prescribed for that offence in the Schedule and in the Schedule appearing opposite the number or letter or both of the section, subsection or clause and the statement of the offence.
>
> SCHEDULE [...] PENALTIES
> H T ACT Reference | OFFENCE | Minimum | Maximum
> 193(1) | Walking on roadway when sidewalk provided | 100 | 500
> 193(2) | Walking on wrong side of road | 100 | 500
> 193(3) | Standing on roadway to solicit rides, etc. | 100 | 500

**What it means:** Hitchhiking from the roadway carries a fine of $100 to $500.
