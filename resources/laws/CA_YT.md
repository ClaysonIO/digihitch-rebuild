# Yukon (CA-YT)

**Status:** Unverified. Yukon's rules of the road are in the Motor Vehicles Act, R.S.Y. 2002, c. 153, which has a Part on pedestrians. I could not retrieve its text in this research session, so this page does not yet say whether Yukon restricts soliciting rides.

## Summary

Every source of the Yukon Motor Vehicles Act text blocked automated retrieval. That includes the official site laws.yukon.ca, CanLII and the Internet Archive. Under this project's rule of quoting only primary text actually retrieved, no Yukon law is quoted on this page. Do not read this page as saying hitchhiking is legal or illegal in Yukon.

What I could confirm:

- **City of Whitehorse:** the office consolidation of the [Whitehorse Traffic Bylaw 2013-34](https://www.whitehorse.ca/whitehorse_bylaws/traffic-bylaw/) contains no provision on soliciting rides or hitchhiking. I searched the full text for "solicit", "hitch" and "ride".
- **National parks (Kluane):** the federal [National Parks Highway Traffic Regulations, C.R.C., c. 1126](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1126/FullText.html) (current to 2026-10-19) contain no provision on soliciting rides. I searched the full text for "solicit" and "hitch".

## Unverified / open questions

- **Motor Vehicles Act, R.S.Y. 2002, c. 153, Part on pedestrians.** Search results suggest it includes sections titled "Pedestrians on roadway" and "Pedestrians crossing roadway". Its wording, and whether it has a "solicit a ride" or "solicit employment or business" clause, still needs to be retrieved from [laws.yukon.ca](https://laws.yukon.ca/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2002/2002-0153/2002-0153.pdf) by someone with browser access. The same applies to the definitions of "roadway" and "highway" and the general penalty section.
- **Freeway pedestrians.** Yukon has no freeways in the usual sense. Whether the Act or any regulation bans pedestrians on any class of highway, such as the Alaska Highway, is unconfirmed.
- **Recent amendments.** A bill titled "Act to Amend the Motor Vehicles Act" (Bill 82, 33rd Legislative Assembly) exists. I could not retrieve it, so I don't know whether it touches pedestrian rules.
