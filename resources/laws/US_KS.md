# Kansas (US-KS)

**Status:** Restricted. You may not stand *in a roadway* to solicit a ride. The roadway excludes the berm and shoulder, so thumbing from the shoulder or curb is not covered by this rule. Hitchhiking and walking anywhere on the Kansas Turnpike are banned by regulation, and drivers may not stop there to pick up hitchhikers.

## Summary

Kansas has the Uniform Vehicle Code rule, K.S.A. 8-1538(a): "No person shall stand in a roadway for the purpose of soliciting a ride." "Roadway" is defined as "exclusive of the berm or shoulder". Hitchhiking from the shoulder or curb of an ordinary road is not prohibited by this statute.

What applies to hitchhikers:

- **Stay out of the travel lanes.** Soliciting a ride from the roadway is a traffic infraction. The uniform fine schedule lists $45, plus court costs.
- **Never hitchhike on the Kansas Turnpike** (the toll road run by the Kansas Turnpike Authority). Turnpike regulations ban pedestrians entirely and ban "solicitation of rides, commonly known as hitchhiking, on any portion of the turnpike." Drivers are also forbidden to stop to pick up or drop off hitchhikers on the turnpike.
- **Other interstates and freeways:** KDOT or local authorities may ban classes of traffic, such as pedestrians, from controlled-access highways. The ban applies where official signs are posted. Disobeying those signs is a misdemeanor.
- **Walk on the sidewalk** if there is one, otherwise on the shoulder. With neither, walk on the left edge facing traffic.

## Laws

### K.S.A. 8-1538: Soliciting rides, business or contributions; exception

**Source:** [Kansas Office of Revisor of Statutes, K.S.A. 8-1538](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch08/008_015_0038.html) (official; history: L. 1974, ch. 33, § 8-1538; L. 1995, ch. 65, § 1)

> (a) No person shall stand in a roadway for the purpose of soliciting a ride.
>
> (b) No person shall stand on a highway for the purpose of soliciting employment, business or contributions from the occupant of any vehicle.
>
> (c) No person shall stand on or in proximity to a street or highway for the purpose of soliciting the watching or guarding of any vehicle while parked or about to be parked on a street or highway.
>
> (d) The soliciting of contributions under subsection (b) shall not be prohibited, if such person or organization has first obtained a permit authorizing such soliciting from the local authorities in their respective jurisdictions.

**What it means:** Subsection (a) covers rides and is limited to the roadway. Subsection (b) covers employment, business and contributions, and uses the wider word "highway", which includes the shoulder. A hitchhiker is soliciting a ride, so (a) is the rule that applies. Thumbing from the shoulder is outside it. Don't offer to pay for or work for a ride with a sign that could be read as "business".

### K.S.A. 8-1459 and 8-1424: "Roadway" and "Highway" defined

**Source:** [K.S.A. 8-1459](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch08/008_014_0059.html) and [K.S.A. 8-1424](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch08/008_014_0024.html)

> 8-1459. "Roadway" means that portion of a highway improved, designed, or ordinarily used for vehicular travel, exclusive of the berm or shoulder. In the event a highway includes two (2) or more separate roadways the term "roadway" as used herein shall refer to any such roadway separately but not to all such roadways collectively.
>
> 8-1424. "Highway" means the entire width between the boundary lines of every way publicly maintained when any part thereof is open to the use of the public for purposes of vehicular travel.

**What it means:** The shoulder is part of the "highway" but not the "roadway". The ride rule in 8-1538(a) stops at the edge of the travel lanes.

### K.S.A. 8-1537: Pedestrians; use of roadways

**Source:** [K.S.A. 8-1537](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch08/008_015_0037.html)

> (a) Where a sidewalk is provided and its use is practicable, it shall be unlawful for any pedestrian to walk along and upon an adjacent roadway.
>
> (b) Where a sidewalk is not available, any pedestrian walking along and upon a highway shall walk only on a shoulder, as far as practicable from the edge of the roadway.
>
> (c) Where neither a sidewalk nor a shoulder is available, any pedestrian walking along and upon a highway shall walk as near as practicable to an outside edge of the roadway, and, if on a two-way roadway, shall walk only on the left side of the roadway.

**What it means:** Use the sidewalk. If there isn't one, use the shoulder. If there's no shoulder, walk on the left edge facing traffic.

### K.S.A. 8-1525: Regulation and restrictions on use of controlled-access highways and facilities; misdemeanor

**Source:** [K.S.A. 8-1525](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch08/008_015_0025.html)

> (a) The secretary of transportation, by duly adopted resolution or order, and local authorities by ordinance or resolution, may regulate or prohibit the use of any controlled-access highway or facility within their respective jurisdictions by any class or kind of traffic which is found to be incompatible with the normal and safe movement of traffic.
>
> (b) The secretary or the local authority adopting any such prohibition shall erect and maintain official traffic-control devices on the controlled-access highway or facility on which such prohibitions are applicable, and when in place no person shall disobey the restrictions stated on such devices. Violation of this subsection (b) is a misdemeanor.

**What it means:** On free interstates and freeways, a pedestrian ban applies where it is signed. Ignoring a posted ban is a misdemeanor (see 8-2116 below), which is more serious than the $45 infraction for soliciting from the roadway.

### K.A.R. 39-1-1 and 39-1-2: Kansas Turnpike, prohibited users; hitchhiking and loitering

**Source:** [K.A.R. 39-1-1](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/kansas/K-A-R-39-1-1) and [K.A.R. 39-1-2](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/kansas/K-A-R-39-1-2) (Cornell LII mirror of the Kansas Administrative Regulations; authorized by K.S.A. 68-2004)

> 39-1-1. (a) Entry upon and the use of the Kansas turnpike by the following shall be prohibited: (1) Pedestrians; [...]
>
> 39-1-2. Solicitation of rides, commonly known as hitchhiking, on any portion of the turnpike is prohibited. Loitering in or about toll plazas or any other portion of the turnpike is prohibited. Stopping of vehicles by patrons for the purpose of picking up or discharging hitchhikers is prohibited.

**What it means:** The Kansas Turnpike is completely off-limits to hitchhikers: no walking, no thumbing, no waiting around toll plazas. It is also illegal for a driver to stop on the turnpike to pick you up or let you out. Get dropped off before the toll plaza, on the public road.

### K.S.A. 8-2116 and 8-2118: Classification and fines

**Source:** [K.S.A. 8-2116](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch08/008_021_0016.html) and [K.S.A. 8-2118](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch08/008_021_0018.html) (8-2118 last amended L. 2025, ch. 7, § 2)

> 8-2116. (a) Every person convicted of violating K.S.A. 8-2503, and amendments thereto, or violating any of the sections listed in the uniform fine schedule in K.S.A. 8-2118, and amendments thereto, is guilty of a traffic infraction.
>
> (b) Except where another penalty or class of misdemeanor is provided by statute, every person convicted of violating any provision of the uniform act regulating traffic on highways designated as a misdemeanor is guilty of a class C misdemeanor [...]
>
> 8-2118(c) uniform fine schedule (excerpt): "Soliciting ride or business on roadway / 8-1538 / $45" [...] "Improper use of roadway by pedestrian / 8-1537 / $45"

**What it means:** Soliciting a ride from the roadway is a traffic infraction with a $45 scheduled fine plus court costs. Disobeying a posted freeway restriction under 8-1525(b) is a class C misdemeanor, with escalating classes for repeat offenses within a year.

## Pocket card

**What's allowed:** You may hitchhike from the shoulder or curb, off the roadway (travel lanes). Never hitchhike or walk on the Kansas Turnpike, and on other freeways obey any sign banning pedestrians.
