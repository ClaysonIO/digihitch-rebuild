# Oklahoma (US-OK)

**Status:** Restricted. You may not stand *in a roadway* to solicit a ride, and hitchhiking anywhere on a turnpike is banned outright. Off the turnpikes, the roadway excludes the shoulder, so thumbing from the shoulder or curb of an ordinary road is not covered by the roadway rule.

## Summary

Oklahoma has two hitchhiking rules:

1. **Ordinary roads (47 O.S. § 11-507):** "No person shall stand in a roadway for the purpose of soliciting a ride, donation, employment or business from the occupant of any vehicle". Oklahoma's definition of "roadway" excludes the shoulder.
2. **Turnpikes (47 O.S. § 11-1401(E)):** "It shall be unlawful for any person to travel a turnpike on foot except to leave or service a disabled vehicle, or for any person to hitchhike on a turnpike." You also may not enter the fenced part of a turnpike except in a vehicle at an authorized entrance.

What applies to hitchhikers:

- **Never hitchhike on a turnpike** (any Oklahoma Turnpike Authority toll road). Get dropped off on a public road before the toll road begins.
- **On other roads, stay out of the travel lanes.** The shoulder is not part of the roadway.
- **Free (non-toll) interstates and freeways:** ODOT or local authorities can ban pedestrians from controlled-access roadways by posting signs. Obey any posted sign.
- **Penalty:** a misdemeanor, fine of $5 to $500 or up to 10 days in jail for a first offense.

## Laws

### 47 O.S. § 11-507: Pedestrians soliciting rides or business

**Source:** [Oklahoma Legislature, Oklahoma Statutes Title 47 (complete title PDF)](https://www.oklegislature.gov/OK_Statutes/CompleteTitles/os47.pdf) (official compilation dated December 31, 2025)

> No person shall stand in a roadway for the purpose of soliciting a ride, donation, employment or business from the occupant of any vehicle; provided, that persons shall not be prohibited from soliciting pursuant to this section if performed on a roadway maintained by a city or town in compliance with a permit and regulations adopted by ordinance.
>
> Added by Laws 1961, p. 380, § 11-507. Amended by Laws 2021, c. 158, § 1, emerg. eff. April 22, 2021.

**What it means:** Don't stand in a travel lane to ask for a ride. On an ordinary road the shoulder is not the roadway (see § 1-158 below), so the shoulder or curb is outside this rule. The 2021 amendment lets cities issue permits for roadway solicitation. In practice that is meant for charity collections, not hitchhikers.

### 47 O.S. § 11-1401: Turnpikes, unlawful acts

**Source:** [Oklahoma Legislature, Oklahoma Statutes Title 47 (complete title PDF)](https://www.oklegislature.gov/OK_Statutes/CompleteTitles/os47.pdf)

> A. It shall be unlawful for any person to enter that part of a turnpike enclosed by fence except in a vehicle at authorized entrances.
> [...]
> D. It shall be unlawful for any person to cross a turnpike except at grade separations provided for cross traffic or through drainage structures under the turnpike.
>
> E. It shall be unlawful for any person to travel a turnpike on foot except to leave or service a disabled vehicle, or for any person to hitchhike on a turnpike.
> [...]
> M. Any person who violates any provision of this section shall, upon conviction, be guilty of a misdemeanor and shall be punished as provided in Section 17-101 of this title.
>
> [...] Laws 2021, c. 243, § 1, eff. Nov. 1, 2021.

**What it means:** This is Oklahoma's strictest rule. Hitchhiking on a turnpike is banned anywhere on it, shoulder included. You may not walk onto the fenced turnpike right-of-way at all. Hitchhike on the public road before the turnpike entrance.

### 47 O.S. § 1-158: Roadway and shoulder (definitions)

**Source:** [Oklahoma Legislature, Oklahoma Statutes Title 47 (complete title PDF)](https://www.oklegislature.gov/OK_Statutes/CompleteTitles/os47.pdf)

> (a) Roadway. That portion of a highway improved, designed or ordinarily used for vehicular travel, exclusive of the shoulder. In the event a highway includes two or more separate roadways the term "roadway" as used herein shall refer to any such roadway separately but not to all such roadways collectively.
>
> (b) Shoulder. The portion of the roadway contiguous with the traveled way for accommodation of stopped vehicles, for emergency use, and for lateral support of base and surface courses.

**What it means:** Subsection (a) says the roadway is "exclusive of the shoulder", which supports hitchhiking from the shoulder. Oddly, subsection (b) calls the shoulder "the portion of the roadway contiguous with the traveled way". A police officer could read that wording against you. The safest spot is clearly off the pavement or behind a curb.

### 47 O.S. § 11-313: Restrictions on use of controlled-access roadway

**Source:** [Oklahoma Legislature, Oklahoma Statutes Title 47 (complete title PDF)](https://www.oklegislature.gov/OK_Statutes/CompleteTitles/os47.pdf)

> The Department of Transportation, the Oklahoma Transportation Authority, or local authorities may, with respect to any controlled-access roadway under their respective jurisdictions, prohibit the use of any such roadway by pedestrians, bicycles or other non-motorized traffic or by any person operating a motor-driven cycle. The Department of Transportation, the Oklahoma Transportation Authority, or local authorities adopting any such prohibitory regulations shall erect and maintain official signs on the controlled-access roadway on which such regulations are applicable and when so erected no person shall disobey the restrictions stated on such signs.

**What it means:** On free (non-toll) interstates and freeways, a pedestrian ban applies where it is signed. Stand at the ramp entrance, before any "pedestrians prohibited" sign.

### 47 O.S. § 11-506: Pedestrians on roadways or bridges

**Source:** [Oklahoma Legislature, Oklahoma Statutes Title 47 (complete title PDF)](https://www.oklegislature.gov/OK_Statutes/CompleteTitles/os47.pdf)

> (a) Where sidewalks are provided, it shall be unlawful for any pedestrian to walk along and upon an adjacent roadway.
>
> (b) Where sidewalks are not provided, any pedestrian walking along and upon a highway shall, when practicable, walk only on the left side of the roadway or its shoulder facing traffic which may approach from the opposite direction and shall yield to approaching vehicles.
>
> (c) [...] it shall be unlawful for a pedestrian to use a bridge where sidewalks are not provided for the purpose of standing or sightseeing.

**What it means:** Walk facing traffic. Don't stand on a bridge without a sidewalk to hitchhike.

### 47 O.S. § 17-101: Misdemeanor violations, penalties

**Source:** [Oklahoma Legislature, Oklahoma Statutes Title 47 (complete title PDF)](https://www.oklegislature.gov/OK_Statutes/CompleteTitles/os47.pdf)

> A. It is a misdemeanor for any person to violate any of the provisions of this title unless such violation is by this title or other law of this state declared to be a felony.
>
> B. 1. Every person convicted of a misdemeanor for a violation of any of the provisions of Sections 10-101 through 14-121 or Sections 16-101 through 16-114 of this title for which another penalty is not provided shall upon conviction thereof be punished by a fine of not less than Five Dollars ($5.00) nor more than Five Hundred Dollars ($500.00) or by imprisonment for not more than ten (10) days; for a second such conviction within one (1) year after the first conviction by imprisonment for not more than twenty (20) days; upon a third or subsequent conviction within one (1) year after the first conviction by imprisonment for not more than six (6) months, or by both such fine and imprisonment.

**What it means:** Sections 11-507, 11-1401 and 11-313 all fall within §§ 10-101 through 14-121. A first offense means a fine of $5 to $500 or up to 10 days in jail. Repeat offenses within a year can mean longer jail terms.

## History

### 2021: municipal permit exception added to § 11-507

**Source:** [2021 H.B. 2238 (enrolled)](https://www.oklegislature.gov/cf_pdf/2021-22%20ENR/hB/HB2238%20ENR.PDF) (Laws 2021, c. 158)

The bill added the proviso allowing roadway solicitation "on a roadway maintained by a city or town in compliance with a permit and regulations adopted by ordinance." The basic ride-solicitation ban was unchanged.
