# West Virginia (US-WV)

**Status:** Restricted. West Virginia bans standing *in a roadway* to solicit a ride. "Roadway" is defined to exclude the berm and shoulder, so hitchhiking from the shoulder is not covered by that rule. Pedestrians can be barred from controlled-access roads by posted signs.

## Summary

W. Va. Code § 17C-10-6(c) follows the Uniform Vehicle Code: "No person shall stand in a roadway for the purpose of soliciting a ride from the driver of any vehicle." Section 17C-1-37 defines "roadway" as the part of the highway used for vehicle travel, "exclusive of the berm or shoulder."

What applies to hitchhikers:

- **Stay off the travel lanes.** The shoulder (berm) is outside the hitchhiking rule. Pick a place where a driver can pull completely off.
- **Interstates and the West Virginia Turnpike:** § 17C-7-13 lets the state road authority (by resolution) and local authorities (by ordinance) prohibit pedestrians on any controlled-access roadway and requires them to post signs. Where signs are up, walking past them is a violation. Stand before the sign at the on-ramp.
- **Walk on the sidewalk** if there is one; otherwise walk on the left side of the road or its shoulder, facing traffic (§ 17C-10-6(a), (b)).
- **Penalty:** a pedestrian violation is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of up to $100 for a first offense, rising to $200 and then $500 for repeat convictions (§ 17C-10-7). The statute provides for fines only, not jail.

## Laws

### W. Va. Code § 17C-10-6: Pedestrians on roadways; soliciting rides

**Source:** [West Virginia Legislature, W. Va. Code § 17C-10-6](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/17C-10-6/)

> (a) Where sidewalks are provided it shall be unlawful for any pedestrian to walk along and upon an adjacent roadway.
>
> (b) Where sidewalks are not provided any pedestrian walking along and upon a highway shall when practicable walk only on the left side of the roadway or its shoulder facing traffic which may approach from the opposite direction.
>
> (c) No person shall stand in a roadway for the purpose of soliciting a ride from the driver of any vehicle.

**What it means:** Don't stand in a traffic lane to hitchhike. Standing on the shoulder or berm is not covered by (c). When walking, use the sidewalk, or the left side or left shoulder facing traffic.

### W. Va. Code §§ 17C-1-35 and 17C-1-37: Definitions of "Street or highway" and "Roadway"

**Source:** [West Virginia Legislature, W. Va. Code § 17C-1-35](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/17C-1-35/); [W. Va. Code § 17C-1-37](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/17C-1-37/)

> "Street" or "highway" means the entire width between the boundary lines of every way publicly maintained when any part thereof is open to the use of the public for purposes of vehicular travel.

> "Roadway" means that portion of a highway improved, designed, or ordinarily used for vehicular travel, exclusive of the berm or shoulder. In the event a highway includes two or more separate roadways, the term "roadway" as used herein shall refer to any such roadway separately but not to all such roadways collectively.

**What it means:** The highway is the whole public strip; the roadway is just the travel lanes. The shoulder and berm are expressly not part of the roadway.

### W. Va. Code § 17C-7-13: Restrictions on use of controlled-access roadway

**Source:** [West Virginia Legislature, W. Va. Code § 17C-7-13](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/17C-7-13/)

> The state road commission may by resolution or order entered in its minutes and local authorities may by ordinance with respect to any controlled-access roadway under their respective jurisdictions prohibit the use of any such roadway by pedestrians, bicycles, or other nonmotorized traffic or by any person operating a motor-driven cycle. The state road commission or the local authority adopting any such prohibitory regulation shall erect and maintain official signs on the controlled-access roadway on which such regulations are applicable and when so erected no person shall disobey the restrictions stated on such signs.

**What it means:** There is no blanket statute banning pedestrians from all West Virginia freeways; the ban applies where it has been adopted and signed. Treat a "pedestrians prohibited" sign at a ramp as binding and stay on the local road side of it.

### W. Va. Code § 17C-10-7: Penalty for pedestrians violating the provisions of this article

**Source:** [West Virginia Legislature, W. Va. Code § 17C-10-7](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/17C-10-7/)

> Any person violating the provisions of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction thereof, shall be fined not more than $100; upon a second conviction within one year thereafter, shall be fined not more than $200; and upon a third or subsequent conviction, shall be fined not more than $500.

**What it means:** Hitchhiking from a travel lane is technically a misdemeanor, but the penalty is a fine only: up to $100 the first time.

## Pocket card

**What's allowed:** You may hitchhike from the shoulder or berm; West Virginia only bans standing in the roadway (travel lanes) to solicit a ride. Obey signs barring pedestrians from controlled-access roads such as interstates.
